ReceiptFlow 1.0.0 — offline Android receipt scanning and expense tracking.

- Vietnamese and English UI, system/light/dark themes.
- Bundled Google ML Kit Latin OCR, crop/rotate and editable receipt parsing.
- SQLite transactions, thumbnails, search, filters and CSV sharing.
- CustomPainter donut and weekly bar charts with interaction and animation.
- Separate demo database and an actual OCR sample image.

Requires Android 7.0 (API 24) or newer. Download `app-release.apk`, verify against `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and install on Android. No account or internet connection is required for OCR.

Physical-device capture and latency evidence are recorded separately in the project checklist. A synthetic sample and emulator run do not establish physical-camera performance.
